Sales

Period-based
Revenue — Current sale totals
Orders — Active (total > 0)
Quantity Sold — Net boxes after returns
Cash Received — Stored cash amounts
UPI Received — Stored UPI amounts

Purchases

Period-based · not subtracted from profit
Purchase Amount — Sum of purchase totals
Purchase Orders — Purchase records
Quantity Purchased — Boxes purchased

Rejections

Period-based · uses saved cost snapshot
Rejected Quantity — Boxes rejected
Rejection Loss — Qty × saved cost price

Expenses

Period-based
Total Expenses — All expenses in period

Profit

Gross = Revenue − COGS · Net = Gross − Rejection Loss − Expenses
COGS — Qty × sale-time cost
Gross Profit — Revenue − COGS
Net Profit — After rejections & expenses

Inventory

Current snapshot · not filtered by date
Total Stock — Boxes on hand
Inventory Value — Stock × Avg PP
Total Designs — Product records
Low Stock — Stock 1–15
Out of Stock — Stock = 0

Receivables

Current snapshot · not filtered by date
Outstanding — Current udhari total
Refund Pending — Current refund_amount
